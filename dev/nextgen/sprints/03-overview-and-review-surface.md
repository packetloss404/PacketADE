# Sprint 3 - Overview And Review Surface

## Goal

Turn FlightDeck Overview into a real supervision and review surface rather than a shallow dashboard.

## Scope

- rebuild overview around attention, review, blocked work, and mission health
- add evidence-backed review packets
- support overview-driven intervention and deep links into mission workspace and sessions

## Team Of 10 Split

1. Overview information architecture - 2 people
2. Review packet model and aggregation - 2 people
3. Overview UI implementation - 2 people
4. Review workflow integration - 2 people
5. Performance and QA - 2 people

## Dependencies

- Sprint 1 complete
- Sprint 2 mission workspace available for deep-link targets

## Definition Of Done

- overview accurately reflects approvals, failures, blocked work, and detached sessions
- review packets contain usable evidence
- operators can move from overview to the exact context needed to intervene or approve

## Risks

- weak aggregation if upstream evidence capture is thin
- performance issues with many active sessions

## Demo Outcome

- supervise several live flights, review a packet, approve a milestone, and jump into the right mission context
